## What it is

MicroMES is a micro Manufacturing Execution System (reduced ISA-95 vocabulary, see [doc/glossary.md](doc/glossary.md)).

## Surfaces and Impeccable mode

| Surface | Path | Mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web app | `apps/web` | **Operate** |

Dense, data-heavy desktop views (tables, forms, dialogs). Scanability, consistency and familiar patterns outrank expression.
