## Reference standard

ISA-95 (IEC 62264) is the reference for vocabulary and conceptual model (Material, Equipment, Personnel, Process Segment, Work Order, ...). It is adopted in reduced form: names and relationships are aligned with the standard, but only what the requirements need is implemented. Every deviation is documented in the feature spec.
